## Production notes

- **One civilization in every plate:** every image prompt repeats the same architecture: pale ochre sandstone with horizontal bands of green-black basalt, tapered towers with stepped crowns, keyhole arches, and a recurring carved spiral-sun relief. The spiral sun ties the shots together: it's on the gate, at the heart of the machine, and in the statue's lap.
- **Episode motif colour:** a lighter rust `#d0663f` for the aqueduct clue line (the missing span of the water channel). Everything else is on the series palette. The amber reconstruction lines and survey rings use series amber `#e9a94b`.
- **Reconstruction:** `ghostLines` paths are hand-placed on the city plate (`plates/hook.jpg`, 1080×1920 coordinates). They complete the collapsed tower crowns and the gateway's top. If the plate is regenerated, the paths must be redrawn.
- **Known trade-off:** in `fall`, the clue line sits behind "THEIR BELIEFS." once the lesson stack lands. The clue reads on its own during "Then ask yourself:" (≈1.3 s), and the broken span and rubble stay visible below the stack.
