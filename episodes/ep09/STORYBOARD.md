# Your Climate Should Affect Your Culture

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 09  
**Lesson:** Climate is a cause, and culture is the answer: clothing, housing, food preservation and seasonal festivals should each be a response to the weather. When people visibly adapt to their environment, the culture feels believable.
