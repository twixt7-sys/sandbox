# Invent a Tradition With a Dark History

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 12  
**Lesson:** A tradition gets interesting when what it means now is not what it meant then. Keep the custom and lose the reason: bells that once found the lost, fires that once kept people alive, a cheer that was once a head count. Write both meanings, and let your characters know only one.
