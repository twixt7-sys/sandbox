# Every Culture Has Something It Considers Shameful

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 13  
**Lesson:** Taboos feel real when each one has a reason inside its own culture. Give two neighbours opposite rules about the same act (a gift, a greeting, a question), and polite people on both sides will insult each other without meaning to. Write the rule and the reason, and the conflict writes itself.
