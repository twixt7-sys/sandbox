# Why Would Anyone Support an Evil Empire?

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 17  
**Lesson:** An oppressive empire survives because many ordinary people hold it up, each for an understandable reason: prosperity (bread, safe roads), stability (order after remembered chaos), self-interest (the wage), fear, and the absence of any better alternative. A villainous empire is believable when its supporters are not villains.
