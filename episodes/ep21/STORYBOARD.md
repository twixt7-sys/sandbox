# The Three Questions Every Magic System Needs

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 21  
**Lesson:** Before inventing a spell, answer three questions: what can the magic do (capabilities), what stops it from doing everything (limitations), and what does using it cost (consequences). Together the three answers immediately generate story: menders fix anything broken, but only what they touched before it broke, and each mend cracks something they own, so when the queen's crown shatters and the only mender who ever held it is a thief, the plot is already there.
