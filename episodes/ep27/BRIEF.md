# EP27 production brief: What Would Dragons Actually Change?

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts, Episode 27 / 50. Season 2, "The World Engine", **Arc 6: Creatures and Species** (second episode).

**Plan entry** (`docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`):
- Hook: "If dragons existed, your world's architecture would look very different."
- Topic: the societal consequences of creatures.
- Core: flying predators change roof design, livestock protection, military strategy, settlement placement and travel. What must ordinary people do differently because dragons exist?
- CTA (a comment CTA): "What would your civilization invent to survive dragons?"

**Deliverable:** a 60.91 s vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps) for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, plus Instagram Reels captions. The AI disclosure is on everywhere.

**Shot list and timings:** [`STORYBOARD.md`](STORYBOARD.md), generated from `episode.json` and the measured `timing.json`. The script that writes `episode.json`, including the whole world timeline, is `make_ep27.py`.

## v2 (5 Nov 2026): its own episode, "the dragon's-eye view"

The user's response to v1 (verbatim): "it seems a bit too same as ep 26. each episode shoul also be unique in its own sense but just with the same level of style andcontent value as ep 26. can you make the episode again? ensure everything looks and feels great, even the content"

**Why v1 felt the same:**
- it re-used EP26's hook pose: the same spot, the same long lens, a creature standing in profile;
- its beats were EP26's beats with a dragon dropped in: RUN, graph, stamp, rewind, checklist, STABLE-style stamp, twist;
- it had the same music plan;
- it had the same kind of shots (orbits and close-ups);
- nothing in it was a *new idea* a viewer could describe in one line.

**What v2 is:** EP27 now has its own device, carried through every beat: **the dragon's-eye view.** A flying predator hunts what it can see, so the episode shows the village *as the dragon sees it*, and every fix is about disappearing from that view.

**The device, beat by beat:** it opens the episode (what a dragon sees), measures the failure (16 targets), scores every fix (the count falling to 00), proves the second run from the dragon's side (NOTHING TO HUNT), and turns around for the twist (the village looks back, and the watcher holds the power). That symmetry is the episode's idea: *a dragon hunts what it can see, so a dragon-haunted society reorganizes around who sees whom.*

**What else is now EP27's own:**
- **Camera language:** EP26 is grounded profile shots and orbits. EP27 rides with the dragon (a new `follow` camera), over its shoulder at the village, for the hook, the lock-on, run 2's proof and the whole ending. The loop rides the same orbit and lands on frame 0.
- **Motif colour:** dragon-sight red `#ff5a4e` (the brackets, the vignette, the PLACE ring). The tower's view uses the series sky blue `#8fc8ff`.
- **Music colour** around the series theme (still D minor → D major):
  - `soar`: horns in open fifths and a taiko wingbeat whenever the dragon is in the air;
  - `raid`: war drums and low horn stabs for run 1;
  - `stealth`: tiptoe pizzicato that layers up as the village hides;
  - the tower bell; a hard rest on "hides".
  - The tempo is 96 BPM (EP26: 100).
- **Sound:** `eyeOn` (a lens sweep as the view switches) and `lockOn` (8-bit lock-on beeps), plus v1's `towerBell` and `burn`.
- **Fresh wording for the recurring beats:**
  - the drop: "They just lived in plain sight", IN PLAIN SIGHT;
  - the checklist: HIDE FROM THE SKY / SKY-PROOF;
  - run 2's verdict: NOTHING TO HUNT, replacing a STABLE-style stamp;
  - the twist: "Look back".
- **The rule for later episodes** is now in `docs/STYLE_S2.md` → "Make every episode its own": one episode device, one camera language, one motif colour, one band of music colour, and fresh wording, inside the fixed Season 2 signature.

Kept from v1: the village layer, the sheep, the field-card sprite, the comment CTA, the cairn tease and every Season 2 signature (the Isle, ▶ RUN twice, the field card with a locked row, golden bookends, the chime at its three moments, the ending formula, the seamless loop).

## The brief

The request: produce EP27 following EP26's patterns, style and vibe, closing EP26's open loop ("a dragon lands here") and growing the Isle by its first village. After the first cut, the user added: each episode must also be **unique in its own sense**, at EP26's level of style and content value.

No people appear and no one is harmed on screen: roofs burn with nobody in them, livestock loss is a falling counter, and "everyone hides" is the carts leaving the road.

**Twist choice.** EP26's §11 offered three twists. *Burns clear farmland* was rejected because it pre-empts EP30 ("a monster that is also the source of the harvest"). *The sky becomes a border* reads mainly as tunnels, which overlaps change 4. *Towers and bells change who holds power* fits the device best: the village learns to *see* the dragon, and whoever watches holds the power.
