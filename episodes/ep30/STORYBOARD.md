# Make a Monster That People Worship

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 30  
**Lesson:** A worshipped monster isn't superstition: it's a deal a society makes with danger. When people depend on what they fear, they invent customs to keep the deal level (tithes, holy ground, first fruits, keepers), and then they need customs to keep the keepers honest.
