# Your World Needs a History Nobody Agrees On

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 31  
**Lesson:** Don't write one true history for your world. Write the event, then write who saw which half of it, what each side gains from its telling, what was lost, and how retelling changed it. You know the truth; your characters only have the pieces, and the gap between the accounts is the plot.
