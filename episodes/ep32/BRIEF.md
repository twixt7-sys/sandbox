# EP32 production brief: Create an Ancient War That Still Matters

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts, Episode 32 / 50. Season 2, "The World Engine", **Arc 7: History and Mythology** (the arc's second episode). Built to EP31's reference standard: start from `make_ep31.py`'s structure, the 25-row scorecard, the camera probe.

**Plan entry** (`docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`):
- Hook: "The war ended a thousand years ago. So why are people still dying because of it?"
- Topic: historical consequences.
- Core: ancient wars can leave disputed borders, inherited prejudices, cursed territories, religious divisions, stolen artifacts and unresolved claims. The past remains relevant when its consequences persist.
- CTA: "What unresolved conflict would your ancestors leave behind?" Used word for word (8 words), as a comment ask: an invention prompt, against EP31's vote.

**Deliverable:** a 58.5 s vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps) for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, plus Instagram Reels captions. The AI disclosure is on everywhere.

**Shot list and timings:** [`STORYBOARD.md`](STORYBOARD.md). The script that writes `episode.json`, including the whole world timeline, is `make_ep32.py`.

## The brief

The request: make EP32 to EP31's standard; close EP31's loop (two war standards rose over the two crowds before Maren's split statue: NEXT RUN · 32 UNFINISHED · A WAR THAT NEVER ENDED); give EP32 its own device, camera language, motif colour (not rust) and music band; tease EP33 ("How Do You Invent a Believable Religion?") with the ending formula; no violence on screen and no real-world religion or conflict; free tools before Figma; spend nothing.

### EP32's own idea: the strata
**The war is a layer in the ground.** The Isle's cut side (the brown cliff under every island shot since EP26) now carries the war as a dark band flecked with bronze-green, and every century since is laid on top of it as a thin band. A war that only *ended* sinks under the centuries and fades into the clay. A war that *matters* sends **veins** up through every layer to the grass, one per consequence. The episode's argument is in the device: *a war stays relevant exactly as long as something connects it to the surface.*

The strata, beat by beat:
- **Frame 0 (today, year 1000):** the war lies ten centuries down with four verdigris veins running up to the grass; a depth ruler reads TODAY at the grass and THE WAR · 1000 YRS DOWN on the layer.
- **The lazy version:** the truce day; the war is the topsoil. Run 1 piles ten centuries on it while it sinks and greys: YEAR 300 · BURIED, YEAR 600 · FADED, YEAR 1000 · FORGOTTEN. The drop: JUST A DATE · 0 LEGACIES · NOBODY CARES.
- **The rewind** peels the centuries off: the war is the topsoil again, vivid.
- **Rules 1–4:** for each consequence the camera goes down to the layer as it is named (a verdigris seed lights in it), then cranes up to what it left on the surface: a line of boundary stones rising down the statue's seam (BORDER), the court's field burning black and staying black (CURSED), the torch leaving the statue's gilded hand for a plinth on the court's side (RELIC), both crowds turning to face the line (GRUDGE). LEGACIES counts 01–04; HEIRS 022.
- **Run 2:** the same thousand years, but the four veins stretch with the sinking layer and keep it tied to the grass: STILL HERE · 1000 YEARS · 4 LEGACIES · 314 HEIRS.
- **The twist:** down at the layer: RULE 5? THE CLAIM, THE PEACE · NEVER SIGNED. On "never", the war's own colour floods up through all ten centuries to the grass while the camera cranes up with it: A PAUSE / IT NEVER ENDED.

The five rules are five of the plan's six consequences, in an order that goes from the land to the people to the paperwork: **a border** (disputed borders), **a curse** (cursed territories), **a relic** (stolen artifacts), **a grudge** (inherited prejudices), and **the claim** (unresolved claims) as the twist. *Religious divisions* is left out on purpose: it is EP33's subject, and the series shows no real-world faith.

**What else is EP32's own:**
- **The camera language: the cutaway crane.** Every pose but the two run wides and the lesson faces the Isle's south-east cliff on one azimuth (az 0, looking due north up the truce line), so the island's cut side fills the bottom of the frame, and the camera moves only up and down: down to the war, up to today. Each rule is a cut down plus a 0.9 s crane up; the twist is a 1.3 s crane up with the flood. The lesson wide is the only orbit. (EP26 orbited, EP27 rode a dragon, EP28 sat at eye height, EP29 was a locked-off tripod, EP30 looked up, EP31 split the frame.)
- **Motif colour:** verdigris `#46c2a0`, the war's bronze turned green in the ground (the veins, the war layer's flecks, the ruler, the STILL HERE stamp, LEGACIES, the field-card helmet).
- **Music band** around the series theme, at 92 BPM:
  - `dirge`: a march slowed to a dirge (the theme at half speed on low horns, a muffled timpani drum on 1 and 3, a snare roll into each bar): the hook, and the war that never ended;
  - `earth`: war drums heard through the ground (a soft taiko heartbeat and a low drone, then a distant side-stick march, a tuba on the roots, the theme far away on muted trumpet), one family per rule.
- **Sound:** `dig` (a vein seeded), `stones` (the line drawn), `rumble` (centuries settling), `eclipse` (EP33's tease).
- **Fresh wording for the recurring beats:** the drop "A war nobody feels is just a date.", stamped JUST A DATE · 0 LEGACIES · NOBODY CARES; the thesis REAL WARS / DON'T STAY BURIED; the checklist THE SEAM WAR · WHAT SURVIVES / DIG IT UP; run 2's verdict STILL HERE; the twist RULE 5? THE CLAIM, THE PEACE · NEVER SIGNED, A PAUSE / IT NEVER ENDED.

**Why "nobody signed the peace".** The hook asks why people still die over a war that ended a thousand years ago. Rules 1–4 answer *how* (what it left), and the twist answers *why*: it didn't end. "It ended in one truce" (said at 5 s, on the field card) is the fair-play clue; a truce is a pause. That also pays off EP31's tease word for word: *a war that never ended*. The writer gets the job: "So hand one character the pen."

**The Isle remembers:** EP31's statue (gilded and tarred, a third taller) and its two standards; EP27's valley (the left horizon), EP29's Queen on her dais (visible, small, at the far left of the cutaway frames, and looking up at the eclipse), EP30's worshipped Grimmaw. EP32's present is a thousand years after EP31's standards rose.

**Nothing graphic, nothing real:** the war appears only as a layer in the ground and as its consequences (stones, a black field, a torch on the wrong side, two crowds facing each other). The field burns with nobody in it. The Seam War, the valley and the court are invented; no real flag, faith or conflict appears (two generated banners that looked like real flags were rejected, §5).

**How it is written** (the rules in `docs/STYLE_S2.md`, measured in §6):
- **The hook** is the plan's question cut to 12 words ("So why are people still dying because of it?" became "Why are people still dying?"), spoken over a frame that already shows the war under the ground and what rises from it.

- **The lazy version is the reader's default** ("the war ends, and everyone moves on"), and it fails for a story reason: "A war nobody feels is just a date."

## 11. How to produce EP33 ("How Do You Invent a Believable Religion?")

1. **Branch:** `claude/worldcraft-ep33` from `claude/worldcraft-ep32` (merge `origin/main` first, keeping both sides). Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/STYLE_S2.md` (the reference-episode note, the writing rules, "Make every episode its own", "Making the next episode", EP32's answer), `docs/SEASON2_RESEARCH.md` §7, `episodes/ep31/BRIEF.md` and this brief.
2. **Plan entry:**
   - Hook: "Don't start by inventing gods. Start by asking what people fear."
   - Core: beliefs help people understand death, suffering, nature, morality and uncertainty. Rituals and institutions emerge around what a society considers sacred.
   - CTA: "What question would the people of your world ask their gods?"
3. **Close EP32's loop.** EP32 ends on "a god built from fear": on "fear" the sun goes dark over the Isle for a breath (an eclipse), the Queen and the Grimmaw look up, and NEXT RUN · 33 BELIEF decodes from an eclipsed sun. EP33's frame 0 could be the eclipse itself over the Isle with both crowds looking up, and the hook's fear named in the world.
4. **Give EP33 its own device** (not an editor, a point of view, a timeline, a balance, chronicles or strata). EP30 already did worship of a real monster with a shrine and a ledger, so EP33 must not be a re-skin of it. One idea: **the fear map**: what the people fear (the dark, the sea, death, the eclipse) drawn as shadows on the Isle, and each belief a light that answers one shadow, with rites and keepers growing where the lights are. Pick a motif colour not yet used (not rust, ink, madder, teal, violet or verdigris), a camera language not yet used, and a music band (e.g. a chant, bells).
5. **No real-world religion** is depicted or named: invented gods only, no real symbols, no real scriptures, no real rites. Check every generated image for real symbols (EP32's banners came back as real flags).
6. **Reuse:** `shrine`, `people.gather`, `people.robe` (EP30), `veil` (what people can't see in the dark), `strata` (a buried god) only as a callback, the eclipse (a `night` light key on a cue).
7. **Writing:** start from `make_ep32.py`'s structure, not its words. Measure with the real TTS (EP32's first draft was 2 s short of the twist and lesson targets), then `python pipeline/benchmark.py ep33 ep32 ep31` and fix every ✗ before rendering a still.
8. **Pipeline notes:**
   - Plan every pose with `pipeline/camprobe.ts`, and render beat stills before trusting it: EP32's probe numbers were right but its first poses were still unreadable until seen.
   - The first still of a session can differ run to run, in either tree; render a throwaway first, and if one regression still differs, render it again in both trees before concluding anything.
   - `loop_span()` and `tcue()` in `make_ep32.py` read the measured timing; re-run `make_epNN.py` after every TTS change.
   - Regression baselines first (`public/` is empty in a fresh container: `pipeline/build.sh ep25 ep30 ep31 ep32` on the branch, then save them).
9. **Then** the same delivery: stills, a `--gl=swangle` render (about 14 min), `qa.py`, encodes, SRT, storyboard, cover, this 11-section brief with the scorecard, docs, a new preview page with the Download button, push.
