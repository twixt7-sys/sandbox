# Create an Ancient War That Still Matters

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 32  
**Lesson:** A war that only ended is a date in your backstory. Write what it left behind instead: a border still drawn, ground nobody will farm, a relic one side still keeps, a grudge each generation is taught. Then decide what was never settled. A war ends when its consequences do, and one character can be the one to end it.
