# How Do You Invent a Believable Religion?

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 33  
**Lesson:** Gods invented first answer nothing, so nobody needs them. Start with what your people fear: death, the lake that drowns their fishers, a hungry winter, a sky that goes dark. Give each fear an answer and let a rite grow around it: candles, lamps, a shared harvest, keepers. Then write the god last, as the answer to the deepest fear of all: that nobody is listening. Every god answers a fear.
