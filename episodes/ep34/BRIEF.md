# EP34 production brief: What If Your World's Creation Myth Is Wrong?

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts, Episode 34 / 50. Season 2, "The World Engine", **Arc 7: History and Mythology** (the arc's fourth episode). Built to EP31's reference standard and EP33's hand-off (§11 of its brief): start from `make_ep33.py`'s structure, the 25-row scorecard, the camera probe, beat stills before the render.

**Plan entry** (`docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`):
- Hook: "Every child learns that the gods created the world. What if the gods are lying?"
- Core: a creation myth can be culturally meaningful without being historically accurate. The gap between what people believe and what actually happened creates room for discovery and conflict.
- CTA: "Would you reveal the truth to a civilization built on a lie?" (12 words). The scorecard caps the spoken CTA at 10, so the video says "Would you tell them the truth, or keep the myth?" (10 words, the same choice made binary); the descriptions and pinned comments keep the plan's full wording.

**Deliverable:** a 57.9 s vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps) for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, plus Instagram Reels captions. The AI disclosure is on everywhere.

**Shot list and timings:** [`STORYBOARD.md`](STORYBOARD.md). The script that writes `episode.json`, including the whole world timeline, is `make_ep34.py`.

## The brief

The request: make EP34 to the standard of EP31, EP32 and EP33; close EP33's loop (on "the gods are lying" a crack of light opened in the meadow south of the lake for a breath, and NEXT RUN · 34 THE LIE decoded); give EP34 its own device, camera language, motif colour and music band, none used before; tease EP35 ("Give Your World a Lost Civilization") with the ending formula; **no real-world religion or creation myth** depicted, named or imitated, no violence; free tools before Figma; spend nothing.

### EP34's own idea: the two skies
**The myth is a painted sky over the Isle; the truth is what the ground shows, and every discovery tears a hole in the paint.** The Lamplight faith's creation myth, the First Lamp ("it fell from the sky, and the world began"), is a painted dome over the whole island: lapis bands, gilt four-point stars, painted clouds, a gold wave band along its horizon, and the First Lamp falling across it with its trail and rays. Seen from outside, its far wall *is* the sky behind the island in every shot. Where the ground disagrees with the myth, the paint tears in a ragged hole with a charred, curling edge, and the true sky shows through: the night void and its stars. Keepers brush fresher paint over the holes, which stay visible as patches. MYTH tags (ultramarine, gilt kicker) are pinned to the painting and GROUND tags (bone) to the Isle, so every rule is a claim against a fact. The episode's argument is in the device: *a myth worth writing has a gap between its sky and its ground.*

The two skies, beat by beat:
- **Frame 0 (today, year 500):** EP33's crack of light lies open in the meadow south of the lake, with a giant's skeleton dug out of a dark trench along it; behind the island, the painted sky is mended with thirteen old patches, and a fresh ragged hole, right of the painted lamp, opens onto the true night. On "lying?" the hole widens with a rip.
- **The lazy version:** the myth is simply true. A perfect painted sky, no holes and no patches; the crack closed and no bones; the cliffs one plain colour (a world made in one night has no layers). The lens lengthens until the painted lamp looms. Run 1 (500 years): nobody digs; HOLES 00. The drop: TEXTBOOK · 0 HOLES · 0 SECRETS · 0 STORIES.
- **The rewind** back to the pristine sky; WRONG MYTHS / BUILD BETTER WORLDS; the checklist TEAR THE SKY with row 5 locked.
- **Rules 1–4,** each a cut to its subject and one dolly zoom on its word:
  - BURY PROOF: on "bury" the crack opens along the meadow; on "Bones" a giant's skeleton rises half out of a dark trench (THE GROUND · BONES · 9,000 YEARS); on "older" the sky tears right behind it (THE MYTH · THE WORLD IS 500). HOLES 01.
  - GIVE IT A JOB: the Queen on her dais; on "Queen's" the camera dollies out to a long lens and the painted First Lamp swells behind her head (THE MYTH · THE GODS CROWNED HER; THE GROUND · HER RIGHT TO RULE). The myth is wrong, and her crown rests on it: that is the stake.
  - LET SOMEONE DIG: the south cliff with a digger and his torch at its lip; on "cliffs" twelve layers stack up the cut side (a callback to EP32's strata; THE GROUND · 10,000 YEARS); on "thousand" the sky tears behind the cliff (THE MYTH · MADE IN ONE NIGHT). HOLES 02.
  - LET SOMEONE HIDE IT: dusk over the valley, robed keepers appear among the crowd (THE GROUND · THE KEEPERS); on "paint" and "holes" both tears are brushed over (THE MYTH · PAINTED OVER), and the long lens makes the patched sky loom. PATCHED 02.
- **Run 2 (500 years):** eleven more holes tear round the painted sky and each is patched half a second later; the last, over the crack, stays open: STILL ARGUING · 500 YEARS · 14 HOLES · 13 PATCHED. The gap is alive.
- **The twist:** RULE 5? NOT LYING; on "remembering" the clock runs back to year 0 and the paint thins until the whole dome is a ghost over the true sky, the painted lamp fading last; the lake's lamp boats withdraw. On "star" a real star falls out of the sky behind the painted lamp into the lake: a ripple, a ring of dust, the lake's rim lit as a crater (THE MYTH · A LAMP FELL; THE GROUND · A STAR FELL, then THE CRATER); every face turns up.
- **"So hide the truth inside the myth."** Day and the paint return; a dolly zoom out brings the painted lamp back over the lake; row 5 unlocks: THE MEMORY · the truth inside.

The four rules and the twist cover the plan's core: **meaningful** (rule 2: the myth does a job, it crowns a queen) **without being accurate** (rules 1 and 3: the bones and the cliffs contradict it), and **the gap creates discovery and conflict** (rule 3: someone digs; rule 4: someone hides it; run 2: they are still arguing five hundred years on).

**Why "it's remembering".** The hook asks "What if they're lying?". The rules answer that the myth is wrong and that its wrongness is useful; the twist answers the question itself: the gods aren't lying, the myth is a memory told wrong. The fair-play clue is planted at 5 s on the field card and in the narration: the First Lamp "fell from the sky", and the sprite is a lantern with a comet's trail. The painted lamp falls across the dome from frame 0; at the twist the real star falls along the same line. It pays off EP33's tease word for word (*the gods are lying*) and gives the writer one job: "So hide the truth inside the myth."

**Not a re-skin of EP33.** EP33 was a faith built from fears and seen from straight above as a map; EP34 is the faith's *story of the beginning*, seen across the Isle at the sky behind it, and its device is not light against shadow on the ground but a painting against what the ground shows. The crack of light is EP33's tease, opened for good.

**What else is EP34's own:**
- **The camera language: the dolly zoom** ("the ground holds, the sky moves"). Every pose looks across the Isle at the painted sky behind it, and every beat is a dolly zoom: the subject keeps its size while the lens changes. The truth dollies *in* on a wider lens, so the painted sky falls away behind the ground (the bones, the cliff, run 2, the twist); the myth dollies *out* on a longer lens, so the painting looms (the lazy version, the drop, the Queen's crown, the patches, "hide the truth inside the myth"). A camera key with `dolly: true` eases the frame's half-height at the target, so the subject holds its size exactly. (EP26 orbited, EP27 rode a dragon, EP28 sat at eye height, EP29 was a locked-off tripod, EP30 looked up, EP31 split the frame, EP32 craned up and down the cut, EP33 looked down at a map table.)
- **Motif colour:** fresco ultramarine `#4a68ff` (the painted sky's pigment, the MYTH tags' frame, HOLES, the STILL ARGUING stamp, the patches); gilt `#e6be5e` heads the myth's words and bone `#e8dec4` the ground's.
- **Music band** around the series theme, at 96 BPM:
  - `lullaby`: the myth as it is sung to children (a harp rocking in triplets, the theme on music box, a warm string pad, a cello on the roots; no drums and nothing liturgical): the hook, the lazy version (in F major, "simply true"), and, in D major, the moment the star falls: the lullaby was a memory all along;
  - `excavate`: the truth coming up out of the ground, one family per rule (a kalimba ostinato with wood-block ticks and a bass; a pizzicato walk over a bowed bass; a low hand drum and a shaker; the theme on clarinet with glockenspiel).
- **Sound:** `tear` (the paint rips), `brush` (a patch), `starfall` (a whistle into an impact), `stoneRise` (EP35's tease).
- **Fresh wording for the recurring beats:** the drop "A myth that's just true is a textbook.", stamped TEXTBOOK · 0 HOLES · 0 SECRETS · 0 STORIES; the thesis WRONG MYTHS / BUILD BETTER WORLDS; the checklist THE FIRST LAMP · WHAT IT HIDES / TEAR THE SKY; run 2's verdict STILL ARGUING; the twist RULE 5? NOT LYING, REMEMBERING, A LAMP FELL / A STAR FELL, THE CRATER.

**How it is written** (the rules in `docs/STYLE_S2.md`, measured in §6):
- **The hook** is the plan's hook in 12 words: "Every child learns the gods made the world. What if they're lying?"

- **The lazy version is the reader's default** (the myth is simply true: the gods really did it), and it fails for a story reason: "A myth that's just true is a textbook."

- **The ask** is the plan's CTA as a binary choice at 10 words; the end card's two chips (TELL THEM, KEEP THE MYTH) make the vote one tap of thought.

## 7. Reusable components added

All are documented in `README.md` (Season 2 building blocks) and `docs/STYLE_S2.md` (motion vocabulary).
- **`world.sky`:** anything a people believes about the sky or the world's frame, drawn as a painting over the Isle that can tear: a creation myth, a heaven, a false map, a propaganda banner over a city, an illusion spell (Arc 8's magic), a simulation's edge. `tears` are any truth breaking through, `patch` any cover-up, `sheer` any revelation, `star` anything falling from the sky (a meteor, a fallen god, a crashed ship).
- **`skyTags`:** paired labels for any claim against a fact (myth / ground, propaganda / reality, map / territory), with `band` to keep a label clear of the HUD while its leader follows a moving pin.
- **`bones`, `ruin`:** a fossil or a giant's grave; ruins rising out of the ground (EP35 can start from them).
- **`dolly: true` on a camera key:** a dolly zoom for any reveal (the subject holds while the world behind it changes).
- **Music:** `lullaby` (a myth, a childhood, something told to the young) and `excavate` (a dig, a discovery, an investigation; `layer` 1–4). **SFX:** `tear`, `brush`, `starfall`, `stoneRise`.

## 11. How to produce EP35 ("Give Your World a Lost Civilization")

1. **Branch:** `claude/worldcraft-ep35` from `claude/worldcraft-ep34` (merge `origin/main` first, keeping both sides). Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/STYLE_S2.md` (the reference-episode note, the writing rules, "Make every episode its own" including EP34's answer, "Making the next episode"), `docs/SEASON2_RESEARCH.md` §7, `episodes/ep31/BRIEF.md`, `episodes/ep33/BRIEF.md` and this brief.
2. **Plan entry** (`docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`): "Give Your World a Lost Civilization"; hook "The ruins are older than humanity. And someone has been repairing them." Check the plan for its core and CTA, and cut the spoken CTA to 10 words or fewer if it is longer (keep the full line in the descriptions).
3. **Close EP34's loop.** EP34 ends "the ruins are older than us": on "ruins" broken pillars (`ruin` prop at (12.8, 13.4), face 20, r 2.2) rise out of the meadow beside the giant's bones and the crack of light, the Queen looks across at them, and NEXT RUN · 35 THE RUINS · OLDER THAN US decodes from a pixel sprite of broken pillars with one fresh stone; then they sink back. EP35's frame 0 could have them standing for good, with one stone visibly newer than the rest (someone has been repairing them).
4. **Give EP35 its own device** (not an editor, a point of view, a timeline, a balance, chronicles, strata, a fear map or a painted sky). Pick a motif colour not yet used (not rust, ink, madder, teal, violet, verdigris, lamplight or ultramarine), a camera language not yet used (not orbit, ride, eye height, tripod, looking up, split frame, crane, overhead map or dolly zoom), and a music band. One idea: **the repair log**, a stone-by-stone record of the ruins where each block shows its age by colour, and the newest blocks (repaired last night) glow; the camera could be a slow *tracking* shot along the ruin's wall (a dolly sideways, never turning).
5. **No real-world civilization or ruin** is depicted or imitated: no real temple, pyramid, ziggurat, henge or script. Check every generated image for real monuments (EP31's first statue read as a real one).
6. **Reuse:** `ruin` (and `rise`), `bones`, `world.sky` (the painted sky can come back torn as a callback), `skyTags` (as claim / fact labels: "BUILT BY GODS" / "BUILT BY …"), `people.look`, `strata` as a callback.
7. **Writing:** start from `make_ep34.py`'s structure, not its words. Measure with the real TTS (EP34's first draft ran at 180 wpm and put the lesson at 42.7 s; Kokoro's length moves by a few tenths of a second between runs, so leave margin on the 45 s lesson), then `python pipeline/benchmark.py ep35 ep34 ep33` and fix every ✗ before rendering a still.
8. **Pipeline notes:**
   - **Plan both ends of every dolly zoom:** a point in the sky at one end can be behind the island at the other. `episodes/ep34`'s scratch approach worked: project dome points with a small pinhole model (the same maths as `camprobe.ts`) and solve for positions that sit in clear sky in every pose that needs them.
   - **Mind the sand:** near-white props (bones, light) vanish on the sand biome around the lake; give them a dark bed (EP34's trench) and check heights (EP34's first bones lay in a low terrace hidden by the next voxel step).
   - **Keep subjects off the caption line** (y ≈ 1,238) in shots with captions on, and keep tags out of the HUD band (y 340–530 with the checklist folded) and the platform-unsafe top.
   - **A night mood darkens the Isle, not just the sky:** with a painted sky, the void behind it is already night; use dusk for a night-time beat so the ground still reads.
   - Colours blended onto the ground are linear (EP33); the first still of a session can differ run to run (render a throwaway first); `loop_span()` and `tcue()` read the measured timing, so re-run `make_epNN.py` after every TTS change.
   - Regression baselines first (`public/` is empty in a fresh container: `pipeline/build.sh ep25 ep33 ep34` on the branch, then save them).
9. **Then** the same delivery: stills, a `--gl=swangle` render, `qa.py`, encodes, SRT, storyboard, cover, this 11-section brief with the scorecard, docs, a new preview page with the Download button, push.
