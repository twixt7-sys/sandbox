# What If Your World's Creation Myth Is Wrong?

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 34  
**Lesson:** A creation myth that is simply true leaves nothing to find. Let yours be wrong about the past and still do a job: bury proof that it can't explain, give it something to hold up (a crown), let someone dig and someone hide what they find. Then hide a real event inside it, told wrong, so a reader can decode it. Every myth is a memory, told wrong.
