# EP35 production brief: Give Your World a Lost Civilization

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts, Episode 35 / 50. Season 2, "The World Engine", **Arc 7: History and Mythology** (the arc's last episode). Built to EP31's reference standard and EP34's hand-off (§11 of its brief): `make_ep34.py`'s structure, the 25-row scorecard, the camera probe, beat stills before the render.

**Plan entry** (`docs/SERIES_PLAN.md`):
- Hook: "The ruins are older than humanity. And someone has been repairing them."
- Core: a lost civilization feels real when its remains reveal fragments of its technology, values, failures and everyday life. Leave unanswered questions instead of explaining everything immediately.
- CTA: "What is the one thing archaeologists should never find in your world?" (13 words). The scorecard caps the spoken CTA at 10, so the video says "What should archaeologists never find in your world?" (8 words); the descriptions and pinned comments keep the plan's full wording.

**Deliverable:** a 59.4 s vertical Short (1080×1920, 30 fps) for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels, plus Instagram Reels captions. The AI disclosure is on everywhere.

**Shot list and timings:** [`STORYBOARD.md`](STORYBOARD.md). The script that writes `episode.json`, including the whole world timeline, is `make_ep35.py`.

## The brief

The request: make EP35 to the standard of EP31–EP34; close EP34's loop (on "the ruins are older than us" broken pillars rose beside the giant's bones for a breath, and NEXT RUN · 35 THE RUINS · OLDER THAN US decoded); give EP35 its own device, camera language, motif colour and music band, none used before; tease EP36 ("Wars Need More Than Two Armies", Arc 8's opener) with the ending formula; **no real-world civilization, ruin, religion or script** depicted, named or imitated, no violence; free tools before Figma; spend nothing.

### EP35's own idea: the finds tray (and the repair log)
**A lost civilization is what its remains can tell you, and one thing they never will.** The Builders left one long broken wall south of the lake. Under the world sits **the finds tray**, a specimen tray with five compartments. The lazy ruin ("ancient, mysterious, gone") leaves it EMPTY: a thousand years of digging and nothing but stones. Each rule digs one fragment out in front of the wall and lays it in the tray, and the four are the plan's four kinds: **tools** (a bronze gear nobody here can make), **values** (the biggest room was a kitchen: one long table for everyone), **failure** (every well is full of sand), **everyday life** (a child's handprints in the mortar). In run 2 every find sprouts two question marks: FINDS 16, QUESTIONS 32. The tray's fifth compartment is locked, and it opens on the twist through **the repair log**: every stone of the wall recolours by its age, the original stone sinking to umber and every repair glowing quartz cyan by how recent it is, and half the wall is newer than us. Night falls, one more stone settles into a gap at the top of the wall, and nobody is there. The compartment opens on WHO? · no answer, and the writer's job is "So never tell us who": the plan's unanswered question, kept.

Beat by beat:
- **Frame 0 (today, year 500):** golden hour, looking north at the wall: EP34's pillars stand for good at its east end, the gear's pit, the kitchen and the sand-filled wells lie dug in front of it, and one stone at the top of the wall glows faintly quartz. OLDER THAN US / WHO REPAIRS IT? is on screen; on "repairing" the stone flares (NEW STONE · LAST NIGHT).
- **The lazy version:** the bare wall (nothing dug, nothing new); ANCIENT / MYSTERIOUS / GONE; the tray slides in EMPTY. Run 1 (year 500→1500): 1,000 YEARS / OF DIGGING / JUST STONES. The drop: JUST ROCKS · 0 FINDS · 0 QUESTIONS · 0 PEOPLE.
- **The rewind;** GOOD RUINS / TALK; the checklist READ THE RUINS with row 5 locked (the tray's fifth compartment is locked too).
- **Rules 1–4,** one slide along the wall, west to east: on "gear" the gear rises out of its pit (FIND 01 · TOOLS · NO FORGE HERE); on "biggest" the kitchen's foundations rise with its long table, benches, bowls and great pot (FIND 02 · VALUES · ONE TABLE FOR ALL); on "well" two wells rise, filled to the brim with sand (FIND 03 · FAILURE · THE WATER RAN OUT); on "child's" two small handprints show in a mortar patch on the wall's face (FIND 04 · DAILY LIFE · A CHILD WAS HERE). Each lands in the tray with a clack.
- **Run 2:** the whole Isle, year 500→1500; twelve dig pits open round the site; 1,000 YEARS / EVERY FIND / +2 QUESTIONS; MORE QUESTIONS · 1,000 YEARS · 16 FINDS · 32 QUESTIONS.
- **The twist:** RULE 5? LOOK CLOSER; on "Half" the repair log lights (THE REPAIR LOG · HALF OF IT IS NEW; NEWER THAN US). Night; on "night" one more stone settles into the top of the wall (LAST NIGHT · ONE MORE STONE); WHO? · NOBODY SEEN; the tray's fifth compartment opens.
- **"So never tell us who."** Day returns, the log fades, row 5 unlocks: A SECRET · never say who.

**Why the twist answers the hook.** The hook states a mystery ("Someone keeps repairing them."). The rules show everything the remains *can* say; the twist shows how big the mystery is (half the wall, one stone a night) and then keeps it, which is the plan's core: leave the question open. The fair-play clue is on screen from frame 0 (the glowing stone) and on the field card (the sprite's top tier is new stone; "Their wall still stands").

**Not a re-skin of EP34.** EP34 was a people's story of the beginning seen against a painted sky; EP35 is the people *before* them, read off what they left in the ground. EP34's painted sky is not over the Isle in EP35 (its device stays its own); its bones, the crack of light and its pillars are on the ground.

**What else is EP35's own:**
- **The camera language: the tracking shot.** Every pose has the same azimuth (az 0: the camera south of the wall, looking north, so screen-x is world-x) and every move is the target sliding sideways: a slow slide west along the wall's top in the hook, station to station for the rules (one slide from the gear to the handprints, no cuts between rules), across the whole Isle for the runs, along the wall to tonight's gap in the twist. ## 7. Reusable components added

All are documented in `README.md` (Season 2 building blocks) and `docs/STYLE_S2.md` (motion vocabulary).
- **`wall` with `log`:** anything whose history is in its fabric: a city wall rebuilt by successive rulers, a ship of Theseus, a bridge patched after every war, a palace with a forged history (a repair log that *lies*). `glow` / `place` are any trace of an unseen hand.
- **`find`:** remains coming out of the ground (`gear`, `kitchen`, `wells`, `hands`, `pit`), for any archaeology, mystery or investigation beat.
- **`findsTray`:** a collection that fills as evidence comes in, with a locked slot for the twist: clues in a mystery, artefacts of a lost magic, evidence for a trial (Arc 8's war crimes, Arc 10's courts).
- **The tracking shot:** one azimuth and `slide()` (in `make_ep35.py`) for any walk past a line of things: a battle line, a border, a market street, a procession.
- **`skyTags` `color` / `headColor` / `head`:** world-pinned labels in any episode's colour.
