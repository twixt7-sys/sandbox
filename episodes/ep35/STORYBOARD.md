# Give Your World a Lost Civilization

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 35  
**Lesson:** A lost civilization that is only ancient and mysterious is just rocks. Let its remains say who they were: a tool nobody can make now (technology), the room they built biggest (values), how it ended (failure) and one small trace of an ordinary day. Then leave one question open and never answer it. Every ruin keeps one secret.

**Voice direction:** The same lab host, quieter this time, like someone walking a dig at dusk. The hook is a mystery stated, not asked. 'Ancient, mysterious, gone' is a dry list of clichés; 'just rocks' lands flat. The rules are crisp, two beats each, with a little wonder on the kitchen and the handprints. 'Half this wall is newer than us' is the chill; 'Nobody has seen them' is almost a whisper. The comment ask is playful.
