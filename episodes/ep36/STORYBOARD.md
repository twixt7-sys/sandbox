# Wars Need More Than Two Armies

**Series:** Worldcraft Shorts · Episode 36  
**Lesson:** A war with one reason (an angry king) is a tantrum, not a war: nobody marches for it. Give every side its own reason worth the cost: something to gain, something to fear, a claim, a promise to an ally. Then let it start by mistake, because every side was sure the others would back down. Nobody thinks they're the villain.
